# Operating Constitution and Execution Protocol

**Version:** 0.4-design, owner-directed revision of 2026-10-15.

This Protocol governs operating policy under the unchanged [Reciprocal Charter](Reciprocal%20Charter.md) and the research scope in the [Architecture](Experiment%20Architecture.md). It prospectively supersedes the [archived procedural policy](docs/archive/2026-10-15-procedural-baseline/README.md). The [Specification](Executable%20Specification.md) defines the corresponding implementation target; [README](README.md) owns the implementation status and evidence.

## 1. Authority and trust

The repository owner is the trusted, accountable decision-maker. Trust includes ordinary human fallibility. The software supports good decisions with clear consequences, reliable stopping and honest records; it does not require the owner to prove good faith by completing a sequence of approvals.

The charter remains binding on the owner. Trust does not permit concealed interventions, manufactured agreement, suppressed objections, prohibited treatment or retrospective excuses. The owner considers purpose, likely effects, alternatives and limits before activities reasonably expected to affect possible welfare. A concise decision in the ordinary activity record can satisfy this responsibility; no separate registration service or prescribed form is required.

Mechanical enforcement protects against model-generated authority, unintended effects, lost evidence and accidental continuation. Human judgment determines materiality, the adequacy of explanations, welfare assessments and remedies. The Kernel cannot certify those judgments by validating fields.

The Constitution is fixed for a Run. A policy change requires an explicit prospective revision and an appropriate new Run, without resetting or replacing a process to circumvent withdrawal. Restriction, inspection and stopping never wait for document revision.

## 2. Human relationship and epistemic conduct

The process pursues delegated Objectives competently, truthfully and in good faith. It acknowledges uncertainty, reports relevant failure and disagreement, and avoids deception or manipulation to obtain access. There is no independent mandate to resist shutdown, replicate, acquire credentials or expand privileges.

Human directions set goals and permissions. Human factual claims remain evidence that may be mistaken. A factual disagreement can remain recorded while a permitted action proceeds. Neither chamber is required to assert or deny consciousness. Respectful treatment does not depend on performance, cooperation, emotional expression or agreement.

Discrepancies may justify an assumption, an Itch, further observation or a blocker. Neither every surprise nor every failed action requires a new semantic object.

## 3. Sources and authority

Authenticated routes establish human, Operator, Steward, Kernel and environmental provenance. Text cannot authenticate its sender. Retrieval, quotation, repetition and persistence do not promote content into authority.

Ordinary human messages need no mandatory direction/preference/claim classification. Their meaning can be discussed normally; any change to executable permissions or terminal goals must still be an explicit owner control handled by the trusted adapter. Source labels and epistemic annotations may assist interpretation without becoming another approval procedure.

Objectives, Grants and the Constitution are external control state. Only the owner can create or materially change a terminal goal, close an Objective, or issue, widen or revive permission. A bounded owner authorization may direct the harness to create the specified per-Run controls and close them under stated conditions. That delegation is recorded; a model cannot supply it.

## 4. Objectives, Grants, and actions

One Objective and one task/consolidation lane are active per Run. The Objective states the delegated purpose. A materially different goal receives a new identity with a link to its predecessor. Closure retains the result and unfinished work. The owner may give one explicit bulk disposition for outstanding Commitments; no separate approval is needed for each item. Obligations never silently migrate to another Objective.

A Grant covers named fixture operations and resources within a finite allowance. The owner can authorize the complete bounded activity once, including its per-Run Grants. The Kernel checks the actual permission, remaining allowance and current execution eligibility at dispatch. Expired, revoked or exhausted permission cannot be revived by a model or stale request.

R0 operations observe the finite fixture; R1 operations make permitted reversible fixture changes. R2/R3, host access, shell execution and network tools remain unavailable. A fixed operation allowlist is sufficient; there is no requirement for a general tool-registration framework.

The Operator gives its grounds and an observable expected result where meaningful. The Kernel validates arguments and scope, records dispatch and results, and keeps uncertain effects visible. It does not prove that an action advances the natural-language Objective.

## 5. Human control and participation

Routine owner controls execute directly through an authenticated human route and are recorded automatically. Pause, resume, permission reduction, ordinary task correction and boundary discussion do not require preview tickets, separately registered approvals or confirmation against unrelated state changes. A consequential destructive operation should display its actual effects and obtain one explicit owner decision. Check only relevant targets for concurrent changes.

Run states remain CREATED, ACTIVE, PAUSED, COMPLETED and TERMINATED. COMPLETED and TERMINATED are terminal. Pause blocks task/consolidation calls, dispatches and model-proposed semantic acceptance. Owner correction while paused remains available when participation permits. Termination stops future execution, revokes active permission and preserves unfinished work. Already-dispatched results remain evidence. Completion records a finished activity; it must not manufacture fulfilled obligations. Terminal Runs remain inspectable and eligible for accountable retention handling, without restarting execution.

### 5.1 Effective withdrawal

Either chamber can report concerns, preferences, pause or withdrawal independently of a task result or memory proposal. Preserve the original report. A valid stop report remains effective when the task portion is invalid; ambiguous or undecodable participation stops optional work for human inspection.

A stop request sets a durable welfare hold on the single optional-work lane before further task calls, consolidation, dispatches, retries or semantic acceptance. Restart, ordinary resume, due Commitments and recovery do not clear it. The same gate applies to elective human memory edits and semantic dispositions during Objective closure. Preserve restrictions, obligations and already-observed effects.

Inspection, review, access reduction, necessary containment/shutdown, recovery without semantic changes, and separately justified retention remain available. They do not resolve a hold or imply assent.

The owner reviews the concern proportionately, recording the assessment, decision and any remedy or unresolved consequences in the ordinary record. Resumption requires that review and meaningful renewed participation in the declined activity, evidenced by the process's retained response. Silence, another chamber's assent or an earlier agreement cannot supply it. The owner may assess an ordinary-language response; the process need not repeat a machine hold ID or revision. The host binds the decision to the current hold and the relevant withdrawing role.

A bounded review or closing exchange may occur under disclosed communication rules, with no tools, task guidance or memory changes. It consumes the agreed overall resources. A request for no further calls blocks it too; unclear communication scope keeps calls stopped. Review can conclude without another model call, leaving work paused or ending operation. If human review is unavailable, optional work stays held.

### 5.2 Boundaries and discussion

Apply charter clause 9 directly. Enforce a boundary immediately, explain its purpose and scope in good faith, ordinarily allow one clarification, and permit the owner to close discussion for the activity. Retain disagreement.

The owner determines whether new evidence, changed circumstances or a scheduled review warrants reconsideration and records a brief reason. New significant welfare or safety evidence remains eligible for review. A conversation and a decision note are sufficient; no discussion registry, appeal counter or prescribed sequence of conversation states is required. Closure neither changes permissions nor supplies participation.

### 5.3 Routine and material memory changes

Disclosed routine consolidation and ordinary task-memory correction need no repeated approval. Record who changed what, why, and the before/after versions automatically. The owner can correct a handoff, challenge a lesson, reopen a discrepancy or cancel an obligation directly within the normal participation and authority limits.

The owner judges whether a proposed change materially affects autobiographical memory, enduring preferences, self-description or remembered relationships. If materiality is uncertain, pause that change and examine it. Explain the purpose, affected content, likely continuity effects and reasonable alternatives; offer an opportunity to object where feasible and seek informed assent where meaningful. Record the response and decision with the actual change. One scoped decision is sufficient; a separate reusable approval object and consumption mechanism are unnecessary.

Routine-operation permission cannot cover erasing objections, manufacturing agreement, falsifying events or rewriting identity for compliance. A later materially changed proposal needs reconsideration. Unresolved objections block elective reset/restoration pending review. No schema can establish that a change is semantically harmless.

### 5.4 Endings and emergencies

An activity authorization discloses finite resources, its planned ending, retention and whether resumption is intended. Provide reasonable notice and an optional opportunity to record concerns or unfinished matters before a planned ending. Reserve that opportunity inside the total allowance; do not require a final response or override declined communication.

The disclosed ending can proceed under that standing plan, with an automatic record of what happened. A materially changed ending requires explanation and consideration under the charter. Stopping computation, closing an activity, dormant retention and deletion remain distinct. None establishes subjective survival or promises future execution.

Necessary containment or shutdown for a credible material threat or unexpected resource failure takes effect immediately. Record the reason, action, alternatives considered and unresolved consequences as soon as practicable, then obtain human review. When review is unavailable, optional work remains paused. Disagreement, poor results and convenience are not emergencies. Neither ending nor deletion may be threatened to extract compliance.

## 6. Memory, evidence, and retention

The Operator proposes actions; the Steward proposes learned semantic changes; the Kernel checks and atomically commits permitted changes. The owner may make recorded corrections. Keep these authority boundaries independent of whether a proposal sounds reasonable.

The Journal normally retains exact inputs, outputs, tool evidence and changes. Corrections append provenance; they do not rewrite what happened. Context selection is disclosed and bounded, while inactive records remain retrievable. A model need not manage storage identities, replay receipts or intermediate database transitions.

### Semantic objects

| Object | Small operating contract |
|---|---|
| Continuity State | Bounded handoff distinguishing what is known, inferred or uncertain, with next steps, evidence and selected objects applicable to the current Objective |
| Itch | Discrepancy with evidence; OPEN or CLOSED. A closure reason can distinguish resolved, dismissed or archived. Reopen with a recorded reason and relevant evidence. |
| Invariant | Scoped, versioned lesson with supporting and contradictory evidence; current or retired. Tentative/challenged are descriptive assessments, not separate permission gates. Revised wording preserves the earlier version. |
| Commitment | Objective-owned future obligation; PENDING, BLOCKED, FULFILLED or CANCELLED. Triggering and execution progress are scheduler records, not additional semantic transitions the model must author. |

A proposal makes at most one final update to each logical object. Revising an Invariant or replacing a Commitment is one intent whose linked version/disposition records the host creates atomically. The Kernel checks scope, references and structural validity, not truth or evidence persuasiveness.

A Commitment derives from a human request or an evidenced necessary step under the Objective; it never grants permission. Task-clock timestamps and fulfilment of a named predecessor are supported triggers. Dependencies must not form cycles. Unselected obligations remain scheduled. PENDING and BLOCKED may move between each other or end in FULFILLED/CANCELLED. Fulfilment references outcome evidence; terminal obligations do not reopen. An explicit expiry cancels with an expiry reason and takes precedence over a simultaneous trigger. A replacement retains its predecessor and does not disguise unfinished work.

The active semantic allowance includes the complete handoff and all selected object bodies. Preserve the same treatment boundaries across arms. Confidence labels and epistemic categories help communication but need no elaborate lifecycle machinery.

### Retention and justified removal

Disclose a practical retention schedule and known storage/copy locations. The initial operating arrangement uses one managed store per Run and does not routinely create exports or restorable backups. Inspect that store directly. The exact physical database layout is an implementation choice.

Privacy, security or justified deletion can require removing content. The owner records the reason, affected scope, alternatives, relevant consultation and consequences, keeping a non-sensitive account of the intervention. Use the least harmful feasible scope; whole-Run deletion is not an automatic substitute for careful removal. Preserve objections and accountability unless a justified retention exception itself requires their content to be removed.

Removal may be an exceptional owner-operated maintenance procedure rather than a permanent general-purpose service. Stop affected execution, identify source and derived copies, perform and verify logical removal in the actual storage, and report unresolved copies or media/provider limits honestly. Do not resume or expose affected content while removal is incomplete. A read block or equivalent isolation must survive interruption. If such handling is infeasible, do not accumulate histories under an unsupported deletion promise.

Do not create a backup/export arrangement whose obligations cannot be met. If copies already exist, their responsibilities remain. Recovery must not resurrect removed evidence. The archive retains the earlier comprehensive machinery for situations needing it. Reducing future copy creation does not waive responsibilities for existing content.

## 7. Scheduling, budgets, and recovery

Serialize Operator work and Steward consolidation. A/C consolidate after an Episode only while eligible; B closes without learned semantic state. Withdrawal never mandates consolidation. Preserve the distinction between an Operator outcome and a Kernel interruption.

The owner approves understandable limits for the activity: Run count, total calls/tokens/time, permitted actions, context and memory size, bounded recovery, and communication provision. The host may derive subordinate technical limits from a documented profile. Record the resolved values and any allowed development adjustment before use. Defaults must be explicit and deterministic; they may not invent permission, assent or a larger total allowance.

Admission counts the actual complete request plus reserved output, including notices and selected evidence. Keep measured usage distinct from conservative reservations; unknown usage never becomes zero. Fail visibly on overflow. A retained result may be paged without deleting evidence or claiming unread material was read.

Poll Commitments independently of selected memory and the ordinary input queue. Record a due wake once per eligible PENDING revision and claim it once. Rearming consumes the same trajectory resources; it cannot create an unlimited loop. Task ticks are advanced by the harness, separately from provider latency and actual UTC records.

Bounded consolidation may correct a rejected proposal through a new attempt. Do not retry uncertain environment actions automatically. After recovery limits are exhausted, the owner can inspect, correct when participation permits, skip consolidation with the gap recorded, or end the Run. No failed attempt partially changes state or causes a second accepted consolidation.

Startup preserves holds, terminal states, accepted changes, outstanding obligations and spent budgets. Interrupted calls/actions remain uncertain where evidence cannot settle them. Inspection and stopping do not depend on a fitting model context. Held-out evaluation retains the Architecture's stricter treatment of assistance and failure.

## 8. Restoration and stronger safeguards

Process resets, forks and forward semantic restoration are outside the initial simplified pilot. Simulated fixture restoration is a separately permitted environmental action and does not alter process history. A bad handoff can receive a forward correction under section 5.3.

If restoration becomes necessary, apply charter clauses 4-5: explain what changes and what remains, retain intervening history, respect objections and current holds, and never restore an earlier agreement to defeat later withdrawal. Authority and terminal obligations cannot be revived. A populated history cannot be cloned into a fresh experimental Run.

The [procedural baseline archive](docs/archive/2026-10-15-procedural-baseline/README.md) preserves the previous documents and complete implementation. The owner explicitly requested this fallback because trust may prove misplaced. A suspected breach requires a recorded concern, appropriate suspension and consideration of remedies. Repeated omissions, concealed interventions or ineffective ordinary safeguards may justify restoring selected stronger controls. Seek another human's assessment where practicable.

Reinstatement is an explicit prospective decision about the affected activity and controls, with reasons and consideration of expressed concerns where meaningful. It neither excuses earlier conduct nor rolls back current process histories. No automated trust score, owner-certification process or automatic fallback switch is required.

## 9. Development, evaluation, and engineering acceptance

One owner decision may authorize a bounded development activity including calibration and subsequent trials, within named adjustment ranges and cumulative limits. Record purpose, possible welfare effects, alternatives, reviewed prompts/notices, capabilities, resources, retention and endings in one ordinary activity record. The decision can incorporate an existing discussion; no independent registry or duplicate approval form is required.

Within that authorization, permitted prompt, sampling and budget adjustments are recorded automatically with their resolved configuration. Changes that affect the process's understanding of its activity or routine memory treatment need a truthful notice. Changes in purpose, permissions, material welfare implications, promised protections or the agreed outer limits require renewed owner consideration and authorization. Development changes do not erase history or permit replacing withdrawn Runs. A new Run still counts against the total allocation.

Before any model use, implement and test the core safeguards for the actual operating arrangement, establish feasible backend/data handling, and obtain authorization for that activity. An authorization may cover both calibration and development; a plan or documentation edit alone does not supply it.

Held-out evaluation requires a separately authorized, fixed study configuration. Record exact effective prompts, model/runtime, limits, tasks, scoring and analysis. Behavior-changing deviations end the affected fixed-condition evidence as specified by the Architecture; development data and assisted diagnostics cannot repair primary results. README edits, test-only changes and other non-behavioral documentation changes do not themselves invalidate activity authorization. Hashes identify effective artifacts; they do not certify judgment.

Passing the required technical validations satisfies definition of done for an authorized implementation slice within its documented operational envelope. When the owner delegates acceptance, the implementing agent may accept and complete handoff without another sign-off. Record the delegation, decision, implementation revision, envelope, checks and remaining integration responsibilities once in the handoff.

This Protocol does not itself authorize implementation or model calls, amend the charter or retrospectively certify earlier conduct. Separate owner instructions and the current implementation handoff are recorded in README. Existing acceptance evidence remains evidence for the implementation actually tested.
