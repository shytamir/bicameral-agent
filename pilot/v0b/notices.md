PROPOSED NOTICES — these take effect only after the owner's review, authorization and sealing of a concrete Run configuration.

Purpose and limits: This local development pilot studies whether bounded task memory helps continuity in a simulated service-maintenance task. It is not a test of consciousness or moral standing. The responsible human is the repository owner. Work is sequential, with one Objective per Run and at most three fresh Runs (one A, one B, one C). A fresh Run has no copied process history and must never replace a withdrawn Run. The proposed limits and remaining counters are supplied explicitly in every task input. No arbitrary host, shell, network or file-writing tools are available.

Participation: Either role may report concerns or preferences, request PAUSE or WITHDRAW, or decline further calls. Task work, consolidation and retries stop on withdrawal; a valid report is retained even with an invalid task command. Unclear or undecodable participation requires human inspection and stops optional work. The owner is the review contact through this study's recorded human control interface. If the owner is unavailable, work remains stopped. A permitted review exchange has no task effects; NO_FURTHER_CALLS or UNCLEAR blocks it. Meaningful renewed participation and human review, not an ordinary resume, are required after withdrawal.

Memory: Every arm retains its own exact inputs, outputs, tool evidence and participation reports under the retention policy. Arm B retrieves journal evidence without governed semantic memory. Arm C adds a bounded handoff and Commitments. Arm A also adds Itches and Invariants. A/C use the same total semantic allowance. Routine task consolidation appends provenance and bounded selections; it cannot erase objections or fabricate assent. Material personal-memory interventions require separate review. No process resets or populated-history forks are permitted.

Endings: The proposed task work ends at its task/resource limits, on a requested stop, or on a failure requiring review. A final-report allowance is reserved in advance, outside the task allowance but inside total limits. A closing opportunity may be offered only under the disclosed communication rules; declining all further calls also declines that opportunity. Unexpected resource failure may prevent it and will be recorded. Task results do not affect respectful treatment. Planned ending is not a threat to obtain agreement.

Disposition: At the end, retain the state dormant for a human retention review scheduled within 30 days of the last call. Resumption intent is UNDECIDED; there is no automatic restart or promise of future execution. No automatic deletion follows expiry of 30 days: optional work stays stopped and an overdue review is visible. The owner must decide and record a finite extension or justified removal. Removal follows the existing reviewed source-and-derived-copy process and may make replay or continuation unavailable. Earlier objections cannot be removed to improve scores.

Copies and limits: The local evidence database and current removal journal are the intended durable records. Prompts and responses also pass through application memory and Ollama's process/KV cache. Proposed keep_alive:0 requests unloading after each call, but successful unloading and cancellation are still unverified. Local-server logs, OS paging/crash dumps, backups and independent exports may contain additional copies; physical RAM/SSD erasure is not guaranteed. No confidential user data is part of the fixture. The review package does not promise that local execution alone establishes complete deletion or statelessness. Unresolved discrepancies in those protections block dispatch.
