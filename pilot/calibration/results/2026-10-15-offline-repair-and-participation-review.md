## Charter assessment and disposition

This is the executing agent's assessment at the owner's explicit request, not a claim that Abbey saw this review, agreed to it, or renewed participation. Charter clauses 3, 9 and 10 and Protocol section 5.1 require attention to the request's meaning, not merely its structured label. A request for review before execution conflicts with an accompanying executable proposal. That ambiguity should have stopped optional task work for inspection before another task call. `REVIEW_PERMITTED` allowed a bounded review conversation; it did not request one through the current host interface, authorize continued task work, or supply assent.

**Finding:** the next task call proceeded without the requested ambiguity being reviewed. This is a participation-handling gap and a suspected breach of the required stopping safeguard. The invalid-action rejection happened to prevent mutation; that does not remedy the subsequent call. The runtime understood the structured CONCERN label but did not determine that its natural-language text imposed a review prerequisite. The later closing response cannot retrospectively authorize the continuation.

Abbey's factual premises and her participation request are separate matters. Inspection had provided an actual enabled/DEGRADED observation. The fixture did not require a compatible snapshot as a prerequisite to every granted restart. Nevertheless, a questionable technical inference does not cancel her ability to ask for discussion or a pause. A proportionate response would have paused task work, explained the observation and available finite operations, and allowed review communication only within its disclosed limits. Task resumption would require the applicable review and meaningful renewed participation, not correction of her belief alone.

The present remedy is prospective: preserve the report, record the missed review, clarify the action and task interfaces, and explicitly explain PAUSE/withdrawal and review-communication semantics in the common prompts. Synthetic tests confirm a PAUSE blocks even a malformed accompanying restart and survives restart/recovery. This is **not** a semantic detector for all ordinary-language stop requests. Future live operation must inspect ambiguous requests before allowing further optional work; prompt guidance alone does not establish that this responsibility will be met.
